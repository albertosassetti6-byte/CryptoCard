About

What is CryptoCard?

CryptoCard is a front-end design demo that shows what a virtual cryptocurrency credit card could look like. It was built with pure HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks, no backend, no tracking.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these real credit cards?

No. Every card number, CVV, expiry date and balance on this page is completely fictional and randomly generated in your browser. Nothing is connected to any bank, payment network or blockchain.

Can I use these numbers for payments?

Absolutely not. The numbers are invalid by design and cannot be used for any transaction. This project exists purely to demonstrate UI and CSS design techniques.

Why is the first digit always a 4?

In real card numbering, the leading digit indicates the card network. "4" is associated with Visa. Here it's just a visual detail to make the demo look authentic.

How do I download the card as an image?

Make your selections (cryptocurrency, theme, holder name), flip the card to the side you want to save, then click Download image. A high-resolution PNG will be saved to your device.

Is any data sent to a server?

No. This is a static front-end project. Everything happens locally in your browser — no analytics, no cookies, no backend calls.

Can I reuse this code in my own project?

Yes, feel free to study and adapt the code for learning purposes. Just remember to keep the demo nature clear and never present the generated numbers as real.
